# Escalation Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** February 20, 2025
**Owner:** Customer Support Operations
**Status:** Active
**Change Summary:** Added edge case handling for after-hours escalations, VIP accounts, and social media complaints.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the escalation procedures for unresolved customer issues within Acme Corporation's support organization. It ensures timely resolution and appropriate involvement of senior staff when standard support channels are insufficient.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customer-facing support representatives, team leads, and support managers handling inbound customer inquiries via phone, email, chat, and social media channels.

## 3. Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — Frontline Support

- **Handled by:** Support Representatives
- **Response Time:** Within 4 hours of initial contact
- **Scope:** General inquiries, account questions, basic troubleshooting, password resets, and billing clarifications
- **Authority:** May issue credits up to $50 and process standard refunds per the Refund Policy

### Level 2 — Team Lead

- **Handled by:** Support Team Leads
- **Trigger:** Issue unresolved after 24 hours at Level 1, or customer requests supervisor
- **Response Time:** Within 8 hours of escalation
- **Scope:** Complex billing disputes, technical issues requiring investigation, repeated complaints
- **Authority:** May issue credits up to $200 and authorize exception refunds

### Level 3 — Support Manager

- **Handled by:** Support Managers
- **Trigger:** Issue unresolved after 48 hours at Level 2, legal or regulatory concerns, or high-value customer impact
- **Response Time:** Within 4 business hours of escalation
- **Scope:** Service recovery for critical accounts, policy exceptions, cross-department coordination
- **Authority:** Full discretion on credits and compensation within departmental budget

## 4. Edge Cases and Special Handling

### 4.1 After-Hours Escalations

Issues received outside business hours (6 PM - 8 AM local time, weekends, holidays) follow modified timelines. Level 2 response time extends to the next business day. Critical system outages bypass this rule and go directly to the on-call manager.

### 4.2 VIP and Enterprise Accounts

Customers flagged as VIP or Enterprise tier in the CRM receive automatic Level 2 routing on first contact. Response time targets are halved for these accounts.

### 4.3 Social Media Complaints

Public complaints on social media platforms are treated as Level 2 by default due to reputational impact. The Social Media Response Team must be notified within 30 minutes of detection.

## 5. Escalation Procedure

1. Document the issue thoroughly in the ticketing system before escalating.
2. Notify the next-level contact via the internal escalation queue.
3. Provide a written summary of all actions taken and customer communications.
4. The receiving party must acknowledge the escalation within 1 hour during business hours.

## 6. Tracking and Reporting

All escalations must be logged in the CRM system with timestamps, reason codes, and resolution outcomes. Monthly escalation reports are reviewed by the Director of Support to identify systemic issues.
