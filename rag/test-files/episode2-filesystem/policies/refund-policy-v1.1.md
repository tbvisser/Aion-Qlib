# Refund Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** March 10, 2025
**Owner:** Finance & Customer Support
**Status:** Active
**Change Summary:** Added exception clauses for service outages, billing errors, and goodwill refunds.

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the processing of customer refund requests for Acme Corporation's subscription services and one-time purchases. It provides clear guidelines for support staff to handle refund requests consistently and fairly.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all products and services sold directly by Acme Corporation, including monthly and annual subscriptions, add-on features, and professional services engagements.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Subscription Services

- Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of initial subscription purchase.
- After 14 days, refunds are prorated based on the remaining unused portion of the billing period.
- Annual subscriptions may be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, no refund is available until the renewal date.

### 3.2 One-Time Purchases

- Digital products and add-ons are eligible for a full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided the product has not been substantially used (defined as less than 20% feature utilization).
- Professional services engagements are non-refundable once work has commenced.

### 3.3 Ineligible for Refund

- Accounts terminated for Terms of Service violations
- Charges older than 90 days
- Third-party marketplace purchases (must be refunded through the marketplace)

## 4. Exception Clauses

### 4.1 Service Outage Compensation

When a verified service outage exceeds the SLA-defined uptime guarantee, affected customers are eligible for a prorated credit or refund regardless of the standard eligibility windows. The credit amount is calculated as:

> (Outage duration in hours / Total hours in billing period) x Monthly subscription fee x 2

The 2x multiplier applies as a goodwill adjustment. Outages must be confirmed by the Engineering team and documented in the incident log.

### 4.2 Billing Errors

Refunds for confirmed billing errors (duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, charges after cancellation) are processed immediately without regard to the standard eligibility windows or approval thresholds. These refunds require only Support Representative approval but must be tagged with the "billing-error" reason code for audit purposes.

### 4.3 Goodwill Refunds

In cases where the customer does not meet standard refund eligibility but has experienced significant inconvenience, a goodwill refund of up to 50% of the disputed amount may be offered. Goodwill refunds require Team Lead approval and must include a documented justification. No more than two goodwill refunds may be issued to the same account in a 12-month period.

## 5. Refund Process

1. Customer submits a refund request via support ticket, email, or phone.
2. Support representative verifies account eligibility against the criteria in Section 3 and 4.
3. If eligible, the representative initiates the refund in the billing system.
4. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
5. Customer receives email confirmation with refund details and expected timeline.

## 6. Approval Authority

| Refund Amount | Approver |
|---------------|----------|
| Up to $50 | Support Representative |
| $51 - $200 | Team Lead |
| $201 - $1,000 | Support Manager |
| Over $1,000 | Finance Director |
| Billing errors (any amount) | Support Representative |

## 7. Record Keeping

All refund transactions must be recorded in the billing system with the reason code, approver name, and date. Monthly refund reports are submitted to Finance for reconciliation. Exception refunds (Section 4) are flagged separately for quarterly review.
