# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** January 27 - January 31, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 295 |
| Open (end of week) | 71 |
| Resolved | 276 |
| Average Resolution Time | 5.1 hours |

Volume increased modestly this week in anticipation of the Q1 product release. A number of tickets were related to customers asking about upcoming feature changes and migration steps. Resolution time ticked up slightly as agents spent more time on complex pre-release inquiries.

---

## Top Issues

1. **Pre-Release Migration Questions (52 tickets)** — Customers who received the Q1 release announcement email had questions about how the new reporting module would affect their existing saved reports and scheduled exports. The team handled these proactively using a prepared FAQ document, but the volume was higher than anticipated.

2. **SSO Redirect Loops (33 tickets)** — A recurrence of the SSO issue from two weeks ago, this time affecting customers using Azure AD as their identity provider. The engineering team identified a regression introduced in a dependency update and rolled it back on Wednesday.

3. **File Upload Timeouts (26 tickets)** — Customers in regions served by our EU data center experienced intermittent upload failures for files larger than 50 MB. The infrastructure team traced the issue to a load balancer configuration that was not updated during a recent capacity expansion.

4. **Dashboard Loading Slow (21 tickets)** — A smaller cohort of users continued to report slow dashboard performance. Analysis showed these accounts had unusually large numbers of active integrations, and the team is working with engineering on a targeted optimization.

5. **Email Notification Delays (17 tickets)** — Some users reported receiving email notifications up to four hours after the triggering event. The email service provider confirmed a delivery queue backlog on their end and resolved it by Thursday.

---

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 9 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 6 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 3 |

The SSO regression accounted for four engineering escalations. Two enterprise customers with Azure AD configurations were unable to access the platform for several hours on Tuesday, triggering SLA-related escalations to account management. One additional account management escalation involved a customer requesting early access to the Q1 release for internal testing.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- The proactive FAQ distribution for the Q1 release was well received. Multiple customers responded to the support team's outreach emails with positive comments about the transparency.
- A healthcare sector customer praised the team for resolving their file upload issue within 90 minutes of the initial report, calling it "enterprise-grade support."

**Feature Requests:**
- Ability to test new releases in a sandbox environment before they are applied to production accounts
- SSO configuration health check tool within the admin panel
- More granular API usage analytics, broken down by endpoint and time period

---

## Team Notes

- The Q1 release support readiness review was completed on Friday. All agents have access to updated runbooks, known issue lists, and the staging environment. The team is prepared for the release rollout starting next week.
- One senior agent transitioned to a team lead role effective Monday, February 3. A replacement hire has been approved and recruiting is in progress.
- The self-service billing sync workflow proposal was approved by the product team and is tentatively scheduled for development in Q2.
- Customer satisfaction score dipped slightly to 4.2 out of 5, primarily driven by the SSO regression and file upload issues. The team expects scores to stabilize once the Q1 release is deployed and the associated issues are resolved.
- The team processed 14 knowledge base article updates this week in preparation for the release, covering new features, changed workflows, and deprecated functionality.
