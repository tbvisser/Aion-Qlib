# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** February 17 - February 21, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 289 |
| Open (end of week) | 68 |
| Resolved | 299 |
| Average Resolution Time | 5.0 hours |

Ticket volume continued to decline toward baseline levels. The open ticket count dropped meaningfully as the team resolved the remaining Q1 release backlog. Resolution times improved as agents became more proficient with the new product features and the knowledge base articles published in recent weeks reduced repeat inquiries.

---

## Top Issues

1. **Billing Invoice Discrepancies (39 tickets)** — Customers on usage-based pricing tiers reported discrepancies between their dashboard usage metrics and the amounts on their February invoices. The billing team identified a rounding error in the usage aggregation pipeline that overstated usage by 2-5% for affected accounts. Corrected invoices were issued by Thursday.

2. **Dashboard Loading Slow (27 tickets)** — The pagination fix for reporting widgets was deployed on Monday and resolved the remaining performance issues for high-volume accounts. Residual tickets this week were primarily from users who had not cleared their browser cache. The team added a cache-clearing step to the troubleshooting guide.

3. **API Endpoint Deprecation Warnings (25 tickets)** — The Q1 release included deprecation notices for three legacy API endpoints. Customers using automated integrations received warning headers in their API responses and contacted support for migration guidance. The team directed them to the migration guide and offered office hours sessions.

4. **SSO Redirect Loops (21 tickets)** — Another recurrence, this time affecting customers using Okta with a specific SCIM provisioning configuration. Engineering identified that the fix deployed in January did not cover this particular authentication flow. A comprehensive patch was released on Wednesday.

5. **File Preview Not Rendering (18 tickets)** — Users uploading PDF and DOCX files found that the in-app preview was displaying a blank page. The document rendering service had a memory leak that caused it to stop processing new requests after prolonged uptime. A service restart and code fix were applied on Tuesday.

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 19 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 11 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 6 |
| Escalated to Finance | 2 |

Escalation volume reached its highest point this quarter. The billing invoice discrepancies drove both finance escalations and three of the account management escalations, as customers demanded formal explanations and credits. The recurring SSO issues generated significant frustration among enterprise customers, with two accounts explicitly threatening to evaluate competitors if the root cause is not permanently addressed. Engineering escalations were dominated by the SSO and file preview issues.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- Customers appreciated the rapid turnaround on corrected invoices, with one noting "mistakes happen, but the speed of correction shows maturity."
- The API deprecation office hours sessions were attended by 18 customers and received uniformly positive feedback. Participants requested that these become a regular offering before major releases.

**Feature Requests:**
- Itemized usage breakdown on invoices showing per-feature consumption
- API versioning with longer deprecation windows (minimum 6 months requested by multiple customers)
- Automated SSO health monitoring with alerts before certificate expiry or configuration drift

---

## Team Notes

- Two temporary contractors joined the team on Monday, providing immediate relief on Tier 1 ticket handling. They completed an abbreviated onboarding and began handling tickets independently by Wednesday.
- First-round interviews for the permanent support agent position were completed. Two candidates advanced to final interviews scheduled for next week.
- The recurring SSO issues have prompted a cross-functional review involving engineering, security, and support. A dedicated task force has been formed to conduct a thorough audit of all SSO integration paths by the end of February.
- Customer satisfaction score held at 4.1 out of 5. While the billing discrepancy impacted scores for affected customers, the strong resolution speed and proactive communication offset broader declines.
- The team is monitoring the escalation trend closely. The increase from 9 escalations in late January to 19 this week is concerning and is being discussed with leadership as part of the monthly operations review.
