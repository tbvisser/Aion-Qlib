# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** February 10 - February 14, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 302 |
| Open (end of week) | 78 |
| Resolved | 307 |
| Average Resolution Time | 5.4 hours |

Volume remained elevated but showed signs of stabilization following the Q1 release. The team made progress on the open ticket backlog, and resolution times improved slightly compared to last week. A significant portion of tickets continued to be release-related, though the mix shifted toward more nuanced configuration questions rather than acute product defects.

---

## Top Issues

1. **Permission Model Changes — Role Configuration (42 tickets)** — Customers continued to seek guidance on the revised permission model. This week's tickets were less about "Access Denied" errors and more about how to properly configure custom roles to match their organizational structure. The team developed a role mapping worksheet that was shared with affected customers.

2. **Dashboard Loading Slow (35 tickets)** — The lazy-loading fix for new reporting widgets was deployed on Tuesday. While it resolved the issue for most users, a subset of accounts with more than 50 active reports still experienced degraded performance. Engineering is working on pagination for the widget data.

3. **SSO Certificate Renewal Failures (28 tickets)** — Multiple customers hit errors when attempting to update their SSO certificates, which is a routine quarterly task for many organizations. The admin panel's certificate upload function was returning a generic error. A fix was deployed on Thursday.

4. **Webhook Payload Backward Compatibility (24 tickets)** — Follow-up from last week's breaking change. While backward-compatible payloads were enabled, some customers reported that the compatibility mode was not activated for their accounts by default. The team manually enabled it for all affected accounts by Wednesday.

5. **Search Results Returning Stale Data (19 tickets)** — Customers noticed that search results occasionally included documents that had been deleted or updated. The search team identified a lag in the index refresh cycle and reduced the interval from 60 minutes to 10 minutes.

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 16 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 10 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 5 |
| Escalated to Product | 1 |

Escalation volume increased again this week. The SSO certificate renewal issue drove five engineering escalations, as customers with imminent certificate expiration dates could not complete the renewal process in time. Account management escalations included two customers requesting service credits due to the cumulative impact of January and February issues. The product escalation involved a formal request from a large customer to participate in a beta program for future releases.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- The role mapping worksheet was well received, with one customer calling it "the most useful support artifact we've received in the past year."
- Customers appreciated the faster search index refresh, noting it resolved a longstanding frustration.

**Feature Requests:**
- Self-service SSO certificate rotation without requiring a support ticket
- Permission model simulation tool that shows what a user in a given role would see before the role is assigned
- Audit trail for all admin-level configuration changes

---

## Team Notes

- The post-release retrospective was held on Monday. Key action items include: (1) establishing a mandatory webhook contract review before any release, (2) creating a customer communication checklist for breaking changes, and (3) adding a "release impact" tag to tickets for better post-release tracking.
- Recruiting for the open support agent position is underway, with first-round interviews scheduled for next week.
- Customer satisfaction score improved marginally to 4.1 out of 5. The team expects continued improvement as release-related issues are resolved.
- The team logged 23 hours of overtime this week to manage the elevated ticket volume and escalation count. Management has approved temporary contractor support for the next two weeks to provide relief.
- Valentine's Day on Friday saw a minor dip in ticket volume, giving the team some breathing room to address the remaining backlog.
