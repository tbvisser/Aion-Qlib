# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** January 6 - January 10, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 347 |
| Open (end of week) | 89 |
| Resolved | 258 |
| Average Resolution Time | 6.2 hours |

This week saw a significant spike in ticket volume compared to the December holiday period. Many customers returned from break and encountered issues that had accumulated over the holidays. Total volume was approximately 40% higher than the trailing four-week average.

---

## Top Issues

1. **Login Timeout Errors (72 tickets)** — A large number of users reported being unable to log in after the holiday break. Sessions that had been idle for extended periods triggered repeated timeout errors rather than prompting a clean re-authentication. Engineering deployed a hotfix on Wednesday that resolved the majority of cases.

2. **Billing Sync Failures (51 tickets)** — Customers on annual plans that renewed on January 1 experienced sync delays between our billing system and their account dashboards. Subscription status showed as "expired" for up to 48 hours after renewal. The billing team confirmed all renewals were processed correctly and the display issue was resolved by Thursday.

3. **Dashboard Loading Slow (38 tickets)** — Several enterprise customers reported dashboard load times exceeding 15 seconds, particularly for accounts with large datasets. The infrastructure team identified a database index that was not rebuilt during the December maintenance window and corrected it.

4. **Password Reset Emails Not Arriving (29 tickets)** — A subset of users on custom email domains reported not receiving password reset emails. Investigation revealed a temporary block by two major email providers due to a sending reputation dip over the holidays.

5. **CSV Export Formatting (22 tickets)** — Exported CSV files contained misaligned columns when opened in certain spreadsheet applications. This is a known issue being tracked for a fix in the Q1 release cycle.

---

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 11 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 7 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 4 |

Most escalations this week related to the login timeout and billing sync issues. Two enterprise accounts with SLA agreements were escalated to account management due to the prolonged dashboard performance degradation. All escalations were resolved or assigned by end of week.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- Three customers sent commendations for rapid turnaround on the login timeout fix, noting that the support team communicated proactively before they even filed tickets.
- One mid-market customer praised the new knowledge base articles added in December, saying they resolved their CSV export issue without needing to contact support.

**Feature Requests:**
- Real-time status page for system health (requested by 6 customers this week)
- Ability to schedule CSV exports on a recurring basis
- Dark mode for the admin dashboard

---

## Team Notes

- The team returned to full staffing on Monday after the holiday rotation. All agents completed a 30-minute refresher on the updated escalation workflow introduced in December.
- Two new support agents (hired in December) completed onboarding and began handling Tier 1 tickets independently on Wednesday.
- Average customer satisfaction score for the week was 4.1 out of 5, slightly below target of 4.3, likely impacted by the high volume of login and billing issues early in the week.
- The team is preparing for the Q1 product release scheduled for late January, which will require updated runbooks and training sessions.
