# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** January 20 - January 24, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 263 |
| Open (end of week) | 52 |
| Resolved | 275 |
| Average Resolution Time | 4.3 hours |

Ticket volume continued its downward trend from the post-holiday peak. The team maintained strong throughput and reduced the open ticket count to its lowest level since mid-December. Resolution time improved for the third consecutive week.

---

## Top Issues

1. **Billing Sync Failures (34 tickets)** — A secondary wave of billing issues emerged for customers on monthly plans whose invoices were generated on the 20th. The root cause was similar to the January 1 renewal issue but affected a different code path. The billing team deployed a targeted fix on Wednesday.

2. **Search Index Delays (28 tickets)** — Customers reported that newly uploaded documents were not appearing in search results for up to two hours. The search infrastructure team identified a backlog in the indexing queue caused by a spike in document uploads from a single large customer. Queue prioritization was adjusted.

3. **Dashboard Widget Configuration Lost (24 tickets)** — Several users found that their custom dashboard layouts had reverted to default settings. Investigation revealed that a database migration on Sunday inadvertently reset widget configurations for accounts created before September 2024. A restoration script was run on Thursday to recover the lost configurations.

4. **API Rate Limiting Confusion (18 tickets)** — Continued from last week, though at a reduced rate. Updated error messages with clearer rate limit information were deployed on Monday and appear to be reducing support contacts.

5. **Two-Factor Authentication Setup Failures (15 tickets)** — A small number of users were unable to complete 2FA enrollment due to a timing synchronization issue with TOTP-based authenticator apps. The security team released a server-side clock adjustment that resolved the issue.

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 7 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 4 |

The dashboard widget configuration loss generated the most customer frustration this week, with two accounts escalating to account management. The security escalation involved a customer requesting an audit log of their 2FA configuration changes, which the team fulfilled within SLA.

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- Five customers specifically mentioned the improved API rate limiting error messages, calling them "clear and actionable."
- A startup customer on a growth plan wrote in to commend the overall product reliability, noting they had zero downtime in Q4 2024.

**Feature Requests:**
- Dashboard layout versioning so users can revert to previous configurations
- Scheduled maintenance window notifications with calendar integration (ICS format)
- Read-only sharing links for reports without requiring recipient to have an account

---

## Team Notes

- Full team attendance this week contributed to the strong resolution metrics.
- Second training session on the Q1 product release was completed. Agents now have access to a staging environment to test the new features before they go live.
- The team identified an opportunity to create a self-service workflow for billing sync issues, which could deflect an estimated 30-40 tickets per month. A proposal has been submitted to the product team.
- Customer satisfaction score held steady at 4.3 out of 5. The dashboard configuration issue pulled scores down slightly among affected users, but proactive outreach and fast remediation limited the impact.
- Planning is underway for the Q1 release support readiness review, scheduled for January 31.
