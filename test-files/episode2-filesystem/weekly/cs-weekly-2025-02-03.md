# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** February 3 - February 7, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 318 |
| Open (end of week) | 83 |
| Resolved | 306 |
| Average Resolution Time | 5.7 hours |

The Q1 product release went live on Tuesday, driving a notable increase in ticket volume. The majority of new tickets were related to the updated reporting module and revised permission model. While the volume was elevated, it remained within the range the team had planned for during readiness preparations.

---

## Top Issues

1. **New Reporting Module — Missing Saved Reports (61 tickets)** — After the Q1 release, a number of customers reported that their previously saved reports were no longer visible. Investigation revealed that the migration script had correctly moved the data, but a UI filter was defaulting to "Created this month" instead of "All reports." A frontend fix was deployed on Wednesday afternoon.

2. **Permission Model Changes — Access Denied Errors (48 tickets)** — The revised permission model introduced more granular role-based access controls. Several customers who had not reviewed the migration guide encountered unexpected "Access Denied" errors when performing actions that were previously allowed. The team directed affected users to the updated admin documentation and offered one-on-one configuration assistance.

3. **Dashboard Loading Slow (29 tickets)** — The new reporting widgets added to the dashboard introduced additional API calls on page load, causing slower rendering for accounts with many active reports. Engineering is working on lazy-loading the widgets in a follow-up release.

4. **Billing Sync Failures (22 tickets)** — Monthly billing cycle renewals on February 1 triggered the same sync display issue seen in January. While the underlying billing was processed correctly, the dashboard showed stale subscription data for several hours. The billing team has prioritized a permanent fix for mid-February.

5. **Webhook Delivery Failures (18 tickets)** — Customers using webhook integrations noticed that event payloads had changed format after the release without advance notice. Several automated workflows broke as a result. The team issued an apology and engineering added backward-compatible payload support by Friday.

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 14 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 9 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 4 |
| Escalated to Product | 1 |

Escalation volume increased significantly this week, driven by the Q1 release. The webhook payload change generated the most heated customer responses, with two customers characterizing it as a breaking change that should have been communicated in advance. One escalation to the product team involved a customer requesting a formal post-mortem on the release communication process.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- Despite the release issues, several customers praised the new reporting module's capabilities, calling it a "major step forward" and "exactly what we've been asking for."
- The one-on-one permission configuration sessions were highly rated, with all six customers who participated giving 5/5 satisfaction scores.

**Feature Requests:**
- Changelog notifications within the product UI so users are aware of changes before they encounter them
- A "release preview" mode that shows what will change before a release is applied to the account
- Rollback capability for permission model changes

---

## Team Notes

- The team operated at reduced capacity with the senior agent's transition to team lead. The remaining agents absorbed the increased workload, though average handle time increased by approximately 15%.
- A post-release retrospective is scheduled for Monday, February 10, to review the support experience around the Q1 release and identify process improvements.
- Customer satisfaction score dropped to 4.0 out of 5, the lowest since October 2024. The team attributes this primarily to the webhook payload issue and the permission model confusion. Recovery is expected as these issues are resolved and customers acclimate to the new features.
- The team created 8 new knowledge base articles covering Q1 release changes and published a video walkthrough of the new reporting module, which has received over 500 views in its first three days.
