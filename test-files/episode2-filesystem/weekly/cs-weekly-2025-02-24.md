# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** February 24 - February 28, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 271 |
| Open (end of week) | 55 |
| Resolved | 284 |
| Average Resolution Time | 4.5 hours |

Volume returned to pre-release baseline levels for the first time since the Q1 release. The team achieved its lowest open ticket count in over two months, and resolution times improved significantly. The combination of contractor support, improved knowledge base content, and the resolution of major product issues contributed to this positive trend.

---

## Top Issues

1. **API Endpoint Migration Support (32 tickets)** — Customers continued to request help migrating from deprecated API endpoints. The team hosted a second round of office hours on Tuesday and Thursday, which were attended by 22 customers. A step-by-step migration script was published on the developer portal and reduced follow-up questions.

2. **User Account Deprovisioning Delays (26 tickets)** — Customers using SCIM-based user provisioning reported that deprovisioned users retained access to the platform for up to 24 hours after being removed from their identity provider. The security team confirmed this was caused by an aggressive session caching policy and reduced the cache TTL to 15 minutes.

3. **Report Scheduling Failures (23 tickets)** — Customers who set up scheduled report exports in the new reporting module found that some reports were not being delivered at the configured times. The issue was traced to a timezone handling bug that affected users in UTC-offset timezones. A fix was deployed on Wednesday.

4. **Dashboard Widget Drag-and-Drop Not Working (19 tickets)** — Users on Firefox reported that they could not rearrange dashboard widgets using drag-and-drop. The issue was isolated to Firefox versions 122 and above due to a change in the browser's event handling. A compatibility fix was included in Thursday's frontend release.

5. **Billing Sync Failures (16 tickets)** — The permanent fix for the recurring billing display sync issue was deployed on Monday. Residual tickets this week were from customers who experienced the issue before the fix went live. No new occurrences were reported after Monday's deployment.

## Escalations

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Escalations | 15 |
| Escalated to Engineering | 8 |
| Escalated to Account Management | 5 |
| Escalated to Security | 2 |

Escalation volume decreased from last week's peak but remained above the January baseline. The user deprovisioning delay was the most sensitive issue, generating both security escalations due to the compliance implications of former employees retaining access. Three account management escalations involved customers requesting quarterly business reviews to discuss the cumulative service disruptions experienced in January and February.

---

## Customer Feedback Highlights

**Positive Feedback:**
- The migration script for deprecated API endpoints received strong positive feedback on the developer forum, with 47 upvotes and several community members contributing improvements.
- A financial services customer commended the rapid response to the deprovisioning delay, noting that the fix was deployed within their compliance reporting window.
- Overall sentiment around the Q1 release has shifted from frustration to cautious optimism, with customers acknowledging the improvements while urging more rigorous pre-release testing.

**Feature Requests:**
- SCIM event logs visible to admin users for auditing provisioning and deprovisioning actions
- Timezone-aware scheduling with preview of next execution time in the user's local timezone
- Browser compatibility matrix published and maintained alongside release notes

## Team Notes

- The final interview for the permanent support agent position was completed. An offer has been extended and is pending acceptance, with a target start date of March 10.
- The two temporary contractors will remain through March 14 to ensure adequate coverage during the new hire's onboarding period.
- The SSO task force completed its audit and delivered a report identifying four integration paths that lacked adequate test coverage. Remediation work has been added to the Q2 engineering roadmap.
- Customer satisfaction score improved to 4.3 out of 5, returning to the team's target level for the first time since late January.
- Monthly operations review highlighted the escalation spike in mid-February as a key area of concern. The team proposed three process changes: (1) mandatory breaking-change review before releases, (2) a dedicated escalation manager role during the two weeks following any major release, and (3) a customer advisory board for pre-release feedback. All three proposals were approved for implementation in Q2.
- The team closed February having handled 1,180 tickets across the month, compared to 1,186 in January. While volumes were comparable, the complexity and escalation rate were notably higher in February due to the Q1 release.
