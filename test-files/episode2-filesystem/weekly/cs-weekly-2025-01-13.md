# Customer Support Weekly Report

**Week of:** January 13 - January 17, 2025
**Prepared by:** CS Operations Team

---

## Ticket Volume

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Tickets | 281 |
| Open (end of week) | 64 |
| Resolved | 306 (includes carryover from prior week) |
| Average Resolution Time | 4.8 hours |

Volume returned to more normal levels after last week's post-holiday surge. The team cleared a significant portion of the backlog, bringing open ticket count down by 28% compared to the prior week. Resolution times improved substantially as the acute login and billing issues from the previous week were fully resolved.

---

## Top Issues

1. **SSO Redirect Loops (44 tickets)** — Customers using SAML-based SSO with certain identity providers experienced infinite redirect loops when attempting to authenticate. The issue was traced to a misconfigured callback URL in our latest deployment. A patch was released on Tuesday and all affected customers were notified directly.

2. **Dashboard Loading Slow (31 tickets)** — Continued reports from enterprise accounts, though at a reduced rate compared to last week. The infrastructure team applied additional database optimizations and confirmed that P95 load times dropped below 3 seconds by Friday.

3. **API Rate Limiting Confusion (27 tickets)** — Several developer-facing customers reached out after hitting rate limits on the reporting API. The error messages returned did not clearly indicate the limit or how to request an increase. Documentation improvements are in progress.

4. **Notification Preferences Not Saving (19 tickets)** — Users reported that changes to email notification settings reverted after saving. QA confirmed the bug is related to a caching layer and a fix is scheduled for the next sprint.

5. **Mobile App Crash on iOS 18 (16 tickets)** — A small but growing number of users on iOS 18.2 reported app crashes during document upload. The mobile team is investigating and expects a fix in the next app store release.
