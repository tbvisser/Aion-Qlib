# Refund Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** August 25, 2025
**Owner:** Customer Experience Division & Finance
**Status:** Active
**Change Summary:** Major revision — simplified refund process, extended eligibility windows, introduced instant refunds for small amounts, removed multi-tier approval for standard refunds.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a streamlined, customer-first approach to refund processing at Acme Corporation. Based on analysis showing that 87% of refund requests were legitimate and that lengthy approval chains increased churn, this revision prioritizes speed and simplicity while maintaining appropriate financial controls.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Acme Corporation products and services, including subscriptions, add-ons, professional services, and bundled offerings across all sales channels.

## 3. Refund Eligibility — Simplified

### 3.1 No-Questions-Asked Window

All products and services carry a **30-day no-questions-asked refund guarantee**. Within 30 days of purchase, any customer may request and receive a full refund without justification. This applies to:

- Monthly and annual subscriptions
- One-time digital purchases
- Add-on features and upgrades

### 3.2 Post-Window Refunds (31-90 days)

After the 30-day window, refund eligibility is assessed on a case-by-case basis. Prorated refunds are available for:

- Subscription services: prorated to the day
- Annual plans: prorated to the month
- Unused add-ons: full refund if feature utilization is below 10%

### 3.3 Professional Services

- Pre-engagement: full refund
- In-progress: refund for undelivered milestones only
- Completed: not eligible for refund, but satisfaction guarantee allows rework at no cost

### 3.4 Ineligible

- Accounts terminated for abuse or fraud
- Charges older than 90 days (except billing errors, which have no time limit)
- Promotional or free-tier services

## 4. Instant Refund Program

Refund requests of **$100 or less** are processed instantly through the automated refund system. No human approval is required. The system validates:

- The customer has an active or recently active account
- The charge exists and has not already been refunded
- The request falls within the 90-day window

Instant refunds appear on the customer's payment method within 1-3 business days.

## 5. Refund Process

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Customer requests refund (any channel) | Immediate |
| 2 | System checks instant refund eligibility | Automatic, under 30 seconds |
| 3a | If instant-eligible: refund processed automatically | 1-3 business days |
| 3b | If manual review needed: assigned to Support Specialist | Within 2 hours |
| 4 | Specialist reviews and processes or escalates | Within 24 hours |
| 5 | Customer notified of outcome with details | Same day as decision |

## 6. Approval Authority

| Refund Amount | Approver |
|---------------|----------|
| Up to $100 | Automated system |
| $101 - $500 | Support Specialist |
| $501 - $2,000 | CX Manager |
| Over $2,000 | Finance Director |
| Billing errors (any amount) | Automated system |

## 7. Abuse Prevention

The automated system monitors for refund abuse patterns. Accounts flagged for review include those with:

- More than 3 refund requests in a 6-month period
- Refund requests immediately followed by re-purchase at promotional rates
- Patterns consistent with friendly fraud

Flagged accounts are routed to manual review regardless of amount.

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|

| Average refund resolution time | Under 4 hours |
| Refund-related churn reduction | 25% improvement over v1.1 |
| Abuse detection rate | Above 95% |
