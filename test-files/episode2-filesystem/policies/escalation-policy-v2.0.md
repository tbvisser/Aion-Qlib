# Escalation Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** July 25, 2025
**Owner:** Customer Experience Division
**Status:** Active
**Change Summary:** Major revision — added Level 4 executive escalation, restructured authority limits, introduced severity-based routing, reduced response times across all levels.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Acme Corporation's tiered escalation framework to ensure customer issues are resolved efficiently with appropriate authority at each stage. This revision reflects organizational changes following the merger of Support Operations and Customer Success into the unified Customer Experience Division.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personnel in the Customer Experience Division, including support representatives, specialists, team leads, managers, and senior directors. It covers all customer interaction channels: phone, email, chat, social media, and in-app messaging.

## 3. Severity Classification

All issues must be classified at intake using the following severity matrix:

| Severity | Description | Examples | Target Resolution |
|----------|-------------|----------|-------------------|
| S1 — Critical | Complete service outage or data loss affecting customer | Platform down, data breach, payment processing failure | 4 hours |
| S2 — High | Major feature degraded, significant business impact | Core workflow broken, billing errors over $500 | 8 hours |
| S3 — Medium | Partial impact, workaround available | Minor feature bug, delayed processing | 24 hours |
| S4 — Low | Minimal impact, cosmetic or informational | UI issues, documentation requests, general inquiries | 72 hours |

## 4. Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — Support Specialist

- **Handled by:** Support Specialists (formerly Support Representatives)
- **Response Time:** 1 hour (S1/S2), 4 hours (S3/S4)
- **Scope:** Standard troubleshooting, account management, billing inquiries
- **Authority:** Credits up to $100, standard refunds, account adjustments

### Level 2 — Senior Specialist

- **Handled by:** Senior Support Specialists
- **Trigger:** Issue unresolved within Level 1 target time, severity upgrade, or technical complexity beyond Level 1 training
- **Response Time:** 2 hours (S1/S2), 8 hours (S3/S4)
- **Scope:** Advanced technical investigation, multi-system issues, escalated billing disputes
- **Authority:** Credits up to $500, exception refunds, temporary service extensions

### Level 3 — Customer Experience Manager

- **Handled by:** CX Managers
- **Trigger:** Issue unresolved at Level 2, regulatory or legal implications, customer churn risk identified
- **Response Time:** 1 hour (S1), 4 hours (S2), next business day (S3/S4)
- **Scope:** Service recovery, policy exceptions, cross-functional coordination with Engineering and Product
- **Authority:** Credits up to $2,000, contract modifications, SLA remediation

### Level 4 — Senior Director / Executive

- **Handled by:** Senior Director of Customer Experience or VP
- **Trigger:** Issue unresolved at Level 3 for more than 48 hours, potential litigation, impact to 10+ customers, media attention
- **Response Time:** 2 hours during business hours
- **Scope:** Executive-level service recovery, strategic account retention, external communications
- **Authority:** Unlimited credit authority, contract renegotiation, executive apology communications

## 5. Automatic Escalation Rules

- Any S1 issue not acknowledged within 30 minutes auto-escalates to Level 3.
- Any issue exceeding its severity target resolution time auto-escalates one level.
- Three or more tickets from the same customer in 7 days triggers automatic Level 2 review.
- Social media complaints with over 100 engagements escalate directly to Level 3.

## 6. Escalation Procedure

1. Classify severity and document all details in the ticketing system.
2. Attempt resolution within your level's authority and timeframe.
3. If escalating, use the automated escalation workflow in the CRM — manual escalations are only permitted during system outages.
4. Include: issue summary, actions taken, customer sentiment score, and recommended next steps.
5. The receiving party must acknowledge within 30 minutes during business hours.

## 7. De-escalation

Issues may be de-escalated when the root cause is identified and a clear resolution path exists at a lower level. De-escalation requires written approval from the current level owner and notification to the customer.

## 8. Metrics and Accountability

| Metric | Target | Review Cadence |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Escalation rate (L1 to L2) | Below 15% | Weekly |
| Mean time to escalation acknowledgment | Under 30 minutes | Daily |
| Escalation resolution rate | Above 90% within SLA | Monthly |
| Customer satisfaction post-escalation | Above 4.2 / 5.0 | Monthly |
