# SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026

**Between:**

**Provider:** CloudMatrix Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider")
**Customer:** Acme Manufacturing Corp., a California corporation ("Customer")

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Service" means the cloud-based enterprise resource planning platform provided by Provider, including all updates, patches, and enhancements made generally available to customers.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, information, and materials submitted by Customer to the Service.

1.3 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents who are authorized to access and use the Service.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of three (3) years ("Initial Term").

2.2 **Auto-Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Termination for Convenience.** NEITHER PARTY MAY TERMINATE THIS AGREEMENT FOR CONVENIENCE DURING THE INITIAL TERM OR ANY RENEWAL TERM. Early termination requires payment of all remaining fees for the full term.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Subscription Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider an annual subscription fee of $240,000 USD, payable in advance within thirty (30) days of invoice.

3.2 **Late Payment.** Overdue amounts shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

3.3 **Price Increases.** Provider may increase subscription fees by up to 15% upon each renewal term with sixty (60) days prior written notice.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 **Provider IP.** Provider retains all right, title, and interest in the Service, including all intellectual property rights.

4.2 **Customer IP Assignment.** Customer hereby assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in any suggestions, enhancement requests, recommendations, or other feedback provided by Customer regarding the Service ("Feedback"). This assignment includes all intellectual property rights in such Feedback worldwide, in perpetuity.

4.3 **Customer Data.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use Customer Data solely to provide the Service.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

5.1 **Data Processing.** Provider shall process Customer Data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Provider's Privacy Policy as updated from time to time.

5.2 **Data Location.** Customer Data may be stored and processed in any country where Provider or its sub-processors maintain facilities. Provider shall provide notice of material changes to data processing locations.

5.3 **Data Breach Notification.** In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting Customer Data, Provider shall notify Customer within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of such breach.

5.4 **Data Portability.** Upon termination, Provider shall make Customer Data available for export in a commercially reasonable format for a period of thirty (30) days. After such period, Provider may delete all Customer Data without further obligation.

## 6. WARRANTIES

6.1 **Uptime SLA.** Provider warrants that the Service shall be available 99.5% of the time during each calendar month, measured excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

7.2 **Customer Liability Cap.** CUSTOMER'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **Provider Indemnification.** Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Service infringes any patent, copyright, or trademark.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Obligations.** Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

9.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was rightfully known prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law.

13.1 **Customer Restriction.** Customer may not assign this Agreement without Provider's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Provider's sole discretion.
